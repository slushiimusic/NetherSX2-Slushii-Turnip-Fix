# v26500720 — Keep framegen running during movement

The FRAMEGEN build no longer treats moving buildings and window patterns as a reason to replace generated frames with repeated real frames. This addresses motion falling back toward the game's original frame rate while Frame Generation still appears enabled.

## Changes

- Reserves artifact protection for abrupt changes across most of the scene with a substantially different color palette. Normal camera movement and dense edges alone no longer trigger it.
- Retains history recovery after a scene change. Generation can resume while the camera is still moving; it does not require a stationary picture.
- Keeps flow scale at **0.25**, the default **2×** multiplier, and support for active **60 Hz or 120 Hz** display modes.
- Includes matching **FRAMEGEN** and **NONFRAMEGEN** APKs. The emulator core, drivers, patches and other settings are retained.

## 30 → 60 FPS setup

Turn **60 FPS Mode OFF**, restart the game, and leave **Frame Generation ON**. A native 30 FPS game can then output approximately 60 FPS, including with the panel set to 60 Hz.

This change removes excessive frame suppression. Some building/window warping may be more visible because ordinary motion is now allowed through interpolation. Ambiguous scene changes with similar colors may also pass through the conservative guard.

## Installation

Install your chosen APK over Pink to retain settings, save states and memory cards. Both variants share the same package and signing certificate and replace one another in place.

## Validation

- Eight shared host checks and two NONFRAMEGEN checks passed. Coverage includes continuous camera pans, full-screen and local moving window grids, abrupt scene changes, and recovery during continued movement.
- On three saved pan recordings, the old detector suppressed 611 of 1,766 sampled frame pairs; the revised detector suppressed none. These are detector replays on recorded composited output, not measurements of newly generated frames.
- Both signed APKs and their variant contents were verified. New on-device gameplay validation is pending.
